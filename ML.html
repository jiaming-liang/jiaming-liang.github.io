Machine Learning, Fall 2026

This course provides an introduction to the mathematical foundations and modern practice of machine learning for computer science students. Topics include supervised learning, regression and classification, support vector machines and kernel methods, unsupervised learning, neural networks and modern deep learning architectures, generative models, diffusion models, and reinforcement learning. The course emphasizes understanding how and why the methods work while keeping the mathematical prerequisites at an undergraduate level.
